Empirical Dataset Observations

Top Feature Importance: Rainfall (22.7%) and Humidity (21.1%) have the highest feature importance in the Random Forest model, reflecting that atmospheric moisture sharply delineates crop viability.
N
Nitrogen Variance: Leafy vegetative crops (Cotton, Coffee) demand high soil nitrogen (>80 mg/kg), whereas legumes (Lentil, Chickpea) thrive at <30 mg/kg due to rhizobial symbiosis.
K
Potassium Outliers: Perennial fruit crops (Apple and Grape) require extreme soil potassium (>190 mg/kg), whereas cereals demand moderate ranges (20-45 mg/kg).
P
Balanced Confusion Matrix: All 22 crop classes achieved >96% individual class recall on the 440-sample test set, confirming no majority class bias.
