Agronomic Limiting Factor Explanation:

A crop's overall suitability score reflects its aggregate alignment with verified historical requirements. The primary limiting factor highlights the specific parameter furthest outside the crop's physiological envelope.

Scientific Note: Suitability scores are rule-based continuous indices computed from historical ICFA agricultural field trials. They are strictly separate from statistical machine-learning prediction probabilities and do not represent guaranteed harvest yields.
