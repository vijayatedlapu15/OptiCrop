Critical Scientific Distinction: Benchmark Lab Accuracy vs. Real-World Field Performance

The 96.72% test accuracy and 90.08% Macro F1 reported above were evaluated on held-out test splits from the peer-reviewed PlantVillage dataset. These benchmark images were photographed under controlled laboratory staging with detached leaves against uniform, high-contrast backgrounds (solid gray/black surfaces).

Real-World Field Reality: In open fields and polyhouses, foliar vision models experience significant domain shift. Variable solar angles, leaf specular glint, wet surface reflections, soil and weed background clutter, leaf curling, and simultaneous multi-pathogen co-infections (e.g., Early Blight paired with Nitrogen chlorosis or Spider Mites) substantially degrade raw convolutional activations.

OptiCrop Safeguards: To prevent overconfident misdiagnoses in real production environments, OptiCrop enforces Shannon entropy gating, softmax probability margin checks, Laplacian texture variance filtering, 14-species host taxonomy compatibility, and mandatory chemical treatment withholding when uncertainty is detected.

Documented Failure Modes & Pathological Limitations

Model Limitations
1. Closed-Set OOD Vulnerability

Closed-set classifiers map all inputs to one of the 38 classes. Unseen foliar lesions—such as European Pear Rust (Gymnosporangium sabinae) on Pyrus foliage—cannot be recognized natively and would force a false prediction (e.g. Corn Gray Leaf Spot) without entropy and margin safeguards.

2. Abiotic vs. Biotic Lesion Mimicry

Potassium deficiency causes marginal leaf scorch in tomatoes that visually resembles Septoria or Early Blight necrosis. Similarly, nitrogen chlorosis mimics viral yellows. Vision models cannot measure leaf tissue sap chemistry; confirmatory soil/tissue tests are essential.

3. Single-Leaf Staging Bias

The model evaluates individual detached leaves without whole-plant architecture, stem cankers, fruit symptoms, or root inspection. Whole-field scouting and local extension consultation are mandatory before executing chemical interventions.
