Model Architecture & Closed-Set Scope

  • Closed-Set Boundary: The classifier is trained strictly on 14 host crops and 38 conditions from the PlantVillage dataset.
  • Unsupported Plants & Pathologies: Foliage from crops outside this set (e.g., Pear, Quince, Rice, Wheat, Cotton) or unrepresented conditions (such as European Pear Rust, Gymnosporangium sabinae) cannot be diagnosed and will trigger out-of-distribution uncertainty.
  • Species Verification: Specifying a plant species checks model compatibility but does not independently verify that the image belongs to that species.
  • Advisory Nature: Optical foliar assessment is an agronomic screening tool, not a substitute for laboratory phytopathological assays (PCR, ELISA, or culture isolation).
